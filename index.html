About

I create with
intention and
atmosphere.

My name is kfr4k. I'm a visual artist and photographer obsessed with light, texture, and the quiet drama hidden in everyday moments.

My work lives somewhere between documentary and fine art — raw enough to feel real, composed enough to feel intentional. I shoot film and digital, always chasing that one frame that stops you mid-scroll.

When I'm not behind a lens, I'm creating content, exploring new cities, and finding beauty in places most people walk past.
